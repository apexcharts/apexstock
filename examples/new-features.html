ApexStock — New Features

A tour of the 0.4 developer-experience additions: a public event API, state serialization, custom indicators (with live streaming), multiple oscillator panes, and indicator introspection. Hover the chart, toggle indicators, save the layout, and press play.

Tick several oscillators (RSI, MACD, Range %) to stack panes. The two custom indicators were added with registerIndicator().

Comparison (multi-symbol)

Overlays other instruments on a secondary axis via addComparison(); percent mode indexes each to its first point.

Annotations (data-space)

Added at price/time coordinates via addAnnotation(); they persist across zoom, theme, and chart-type switches.

Drawings (programmatic)

Anchored to price/time via addDrawing(); they re-project through zoom/pan and round-trip through getState().
